Games

Simple games where a coherent heart quietly helps: time slows, the light widens, the aim steadies. A scattered heart never makes a game harder than normal. Playing records a session, like breathing with a scene does.

The current scene stays behind the game, dimmed (in full graphics). Your heart source and breathing pace stay as they are. With practice mode, hold Space while you breathe in, or on a phone the round pad in the corner.

Breathing pace

Set your own timing. Changes apply straight away. Many people settle best somewhere between 4.5 and 7 breaths a minute, often with a slightly longer exhale.

Difficulty

On: after each sensor session of 3 minutes or more, the low / medium / high thresholds move so that you spend about half your time in high. A rank you reach holds through a grey zone and only eases down after 7 weak sessions in a row. Rank badges stay forever.

Graphics

Colour

With a tint on, the whole scene shifts toward the colour of your current level, dims when you are scattered and glows when you are coherent. Brightness carries the meaning on its own, so the colour-blind safe set works without telling hues apart. C switches the tint.

With the emWave connected you always see both: Breathfield's score at the top, the emWave's colour on the line below. Every session keeps both, which is what calibration uses.

Sessions

Everything here applies straight away, with two exceptions that keep a running session fair: session length and the difficulty settings (adapting, fixed rank) take effect from the next session.
